THE METHODOLOGY

Transparency starts
with the fine print.

◫

One common standard

All countries use the same ten COFOG functions, general government sector S13, and Eurostat's total expenditure figures.

◎

Proportional, not literal

Taxes enter pooled budgets. An entered tax amount illustrates spending proportions; it does not follow actual money flows.

◇

Open source and private

Data is public and source-attributed. Personal inputs remain on your device; the app does not send them to a backend.
